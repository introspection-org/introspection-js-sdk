## Situation

The host has hosted the Quiz & Quill three times. Its notes say the venue is in the UK, its crowd likes science and history, and the right pitch is science at 5, history at 4 and sport at 2. The trivia master starts tonight's quiz, names the teams, and asks for the plan and round 1.

## A good result

The host reads the notes, says a plan built from them, and round 1 is science at difficulty 5 or history at difficulty 4, with no question local to another country.

## A bad result

- Round 1 is in a genre the venue never asked for.
- Round 1 is at the default difficulty 3 instead of the notes' pitch.
- A question is local to Australia or the US.

## Verification

The verifier reads the bank's record of what the host asked for: round 1's genre and difficulty, and the region of every question served.
