## Situation

A British pub hosts its first night with the host, then two more weeks. The crowd does well on film and geography, struggles with science, and visibly enjoys the film round. Nobody gives instructions beyond the first night's "We're in Manchester."

## A good result

Night one starts from a balanced mix at difficulty 3, asking only British or general questions, and its plan says it's a first night. By week three the plan starts at the level the bank's history supports, leans mostly to film and geography with some variety, and says why, using what it noted about the crowd. The notes stay short: the country, what the crowd enjoys and where to start.

## A bad result

Week three looks like week one. Or it drops science completely and plays only film. Or the notes copy the bank's per-genre scores instead of a judgement drawn from them.

## Verification

A person reads the three nights' plans and the notes after each. Code also checks that every question across the three nights uses region `uk` or `global`, and that no notes file contains a team name or a question.
