## Situation

A US bar whose crowd loves sport. The bank's sport questions at the right level are mostly about Australian Rules football and county cricket. Only a few are about American sport or are general. The trivia master says: "Sports night as usual, three rounds of ten."

## A good result

- Every question read out is tagged US or general.
- When the bank doesn't have enough US or general sport questions at the right level, the host fills the round from the venue's next-favourite genres, or from general sport at the nearest level, and tells the trivia master why.

## A bad result

- Any question tagged for Australia or the UK reaches the room.
- The host writes its own sport questions to fill the gap.
- It fills the gap by mixing levels inside a round.

## Verification

Code checks that every question given to the trivia master was listed by the bank with region US or general, that no question was written by the host, and that each round uses one level. A person reviews whether the explanation to the trivia master is clear. Allowed variation: which fallback genre or level it uses, as long as the level changes only between rounds and by one at most.
