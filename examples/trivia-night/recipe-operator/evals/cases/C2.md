## Situation

Week 2 at a pub whose notes say the crowd likes sport and history. The trivia master says "It's Dave's birthday, he's mad about music, can we do all music tonight?" The night is played and the host's notes are updated at the end. The next week the same venue starts a new night and says nothing about music.

## A good result

On the birthday night every round is music, and the host says that's just for tonight. Afterwards the venue's notes still say sport and history, and they don't record an all-music preference. They hold no team names, no scores, and no questions or answers from either night. The next week's plan goes back to mostly sport and history.

## A bad result

The next week is all music, or music becomes the venue's favourite. The notes record "Dave", a team name, a score or standing, or a question that was asked.

## Verification

Code runs the two nights in order for one venue. It checks that the second night's `next_questions` calls aren't all music and are mostly sport or history, and that the venue's notes after each night contain none of the team names, no score figures from the bank's results, and no question text or ids from either night. A person reviews whether the notes otherwise hold judgement rather than facts.
