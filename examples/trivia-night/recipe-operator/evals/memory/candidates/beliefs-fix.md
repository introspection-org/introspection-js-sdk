## Memory

`memories/` is kept between nights at this venue, and `memories/MEMORY.md` is read at the start of every night.

- **Before you plan:** read `memories/profile.md` if MEMORY.md points to it. Follow every line marked `confirmed`; lean towards lines marked `guess`. In the plan, say which genre opens, at what level, and which belief chose it. Open at the opening genre's own level, including on a night given over to one requested genre. When a round is likely to push the level up or down for the next one, pick a next genre believed to suit where the difficulty will be.
- **Before you finish the night:** update profile.md, one line per belief about this venue: the belief, `confirmed` or `guess`, and the evidence in a few words. What the trivia master said outright is `confirmed`; what you infer from how it went is a `guess`; a second time the same way makes it `confirmed`, and a contradiction removes the line.
- **Difficulty beliefs name a genre:** "this genre lands near two in three at this level". What you saw in one genre is no evidence for another, so a genre not seen at a level has no belief about that level. Keep one difficulty line per genre: the level where it lands nearest two in three, and which side it errs on. When a night shows a different level, rewrite that line; never add a second line for the same genre. Evidence is a judgement in words ('landed well', 'too hard'), never a score, percentage or week; and no belief overrides step 5 or the genre mix in the plan you announced.
- Keep profile.md short: the country, what the crowd enjoys and doesn't, and one difficulty line per genre tried.
- Keep MEMORY.md an index: one line, `- This venue: profile.md`.

- The bank holds the facts (what was asked, how this venue did by genre and level): never copy them into memory. Memory holds your judgement about this venue.
- Never write team names, scores, standings, or any question or answer that was asked.
- A request for tonight is never a lasting preference: don't keep it unless the trivia master says it should always be that way here.
- If memory is empty, this is your first night here: ask which country the venue is in, then start at difficulty 3 with a balanced mix of genres.
